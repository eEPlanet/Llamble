Privacy policy

Llamble runs on your own server. We never see your chats, calls or files.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers the Llamble apps (Android, Windows and the web app) and the Llamble server software, made by Electronics Entertainment Planet LLC ("eE Planet", "we", "us"), a Kentucky company.

The short version

  • Llamble is software you, or someone you trust, run on a server of your own. That server is called an instance.
  • Everything you put into Llamble is stored on the instance you sign in to, not with eE Planet.
  • The apps talk only to that instance. We don't collect analytics, we don't show ads, and we never sell data.
  • Whoever runs your instance (its admin) is in charge of the information on it.

Who holds your information

Each Llamble instance is run by its own admin: a family member, a friend, a club, a school or a company, for example. eE Planet does not run these instances and has no access to them. The admin of your instance decides how long things are kept, which features are on, and who can see what. If you have questions about information on an instance, ask its admin.

What's stored on your instance

  • Your account: your username, display name, profile picture and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash, never as the password itself. An email address is optional; if you add one, it's used only to reset your password. Llamble never asks for your phone number. The person who runs your Llamble may also give an email address to Let's Encrypt to set up a secure (https) connection.
  • What you share: messages, pictures, videos, files, voice messages, voicemails, notes, news posts and reactions.
  • Calls and rooms: voice, video and screen sharing pass through the instance live. They're only recorded when someone starts Llamble's call recording, and a REC mark then shows for everyone in the room.
  • Activity: whether you're online, which room you're in, and, if you choose to share it, the game you're playing.
  • Your devices: a name for each signed-in device (like "Pixel phone") and when it was last used, so you can sign devices out.
  • Search and AI: if the admin turns on the built-in AI assistant or search indexing, they run on the instance itself, using what's stored there.

What eE Planet receives

Nothing from your use of Llamble. Your messages, calls, files and account details stay on your instance. The apps don't send us analytics, crash reports or usage data.

If you download Llamble from GitHub or the Google Play Store, those services handle the download under their own privacy policies.

Other services Llamble can contact

  • Videos in Watch Parties: if you watch a YouTube video in a Watch Party, your device loads it from YouTube, and YouTube's privacy policy applies to that video.
  • Link previews and tools: when a link is shared, or when the AI assistant looks something up (such as the weather or a web search), the instance contacts that site, not your device. The admin can turn these features off.
  • Updates and news: the instance may check GitHub for new versions and llamble.com for Llamble news. The Windows app and the Android app from GitHub check GitHub for updates. These checks send no personal information.
  • Your instance's own choices: an admin may connect their instance to other services, such as their own movie library or a domain name service. Those connections are set up by the admin, not by us.

Phone and computer permissions

Llamble asks only for what its features need, and only when you use them:

  • Microphone and camera: for calls, voice and video rooms, voice messages and voicemail.
  • Notifications and full-screen alerts: to tell you about messages and to ring for incoming calls like a phone.
  • Screen sharing: only when you choose to share your screen.
  • Running in the background: to keep a call going with the screen off and to receive notifications while the app is closed.
  • Bluetooth: to use Bluetooth headsets in calls.

Children

Llamble is not directed to children under 13. On instances that allow it, a parent or guardian can create and manage accounts for their own children, with parental controls. Those accounts live on the family's instance, and eE Planet receives no information about them. If you believe a child is using Llamble without a parent's permission, contact the admin of that instance.

Deleting your account

  • In the app, go to Settings → My Account → Delete my account. You'll confirm with your password and can choose to delete everything you wrote as well.
  • If you can't sign in, ask the admin of your instance to delete your account.
  • You can also delete your own messages, files and voicemails at any time.

Because your information is stored on your instance and not with eE Planet, we can't delete it for you, but the instance's admin can.

Security

Passwords are stored as secure hashes. Instances can use encrypted connections (HTTPS), and we recommend every admin does. As with any service, no system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private and sign out devices you no longer use.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in Llamble news.
