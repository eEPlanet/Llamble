Servers and who gets in

Each server decides how people join, and who looks after it.

🚪 How people join

  • Open: anyone can find it and join
  • Look around, then request: people can read it first, then ask to join
  • Request only: people ask to join, and staff say yes or no
  • Invite only: hidden from the list. Staff send invites

🔭 Finding servers

  • The binoculars list the open servers on your Llamble
  • Staff answer requests; after a no, you can ask again in 7 days

🧑‍✈️ Staff

  • Built-in roles: Admin, Supervisor, Moderator and Guest
  • Make your own roles with any mix of powers
  • Kick and ban

🏠 Your instance

  • The instance admin runs the whole Llamble: accounts, settings, updates
  • Choose who can create servers
