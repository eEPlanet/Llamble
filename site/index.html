Everything at a glance

Home shows who's in a room right now, your notifications, news and notes from all your servers. One tap to join.

Watch parties

A shared player that stays in sync for everyone, with voice and chat beside it. Queue YouTube, links and streams, movies uploaded to your server, your own Jellyfin library, or a video from your device. Posters and subtitles included.

Friends and parties

See who's online and what they're doing, jump into their room, call them, or start a party: a small group with its own chat and voice.

Calls that ring, and voicemail

Calls ring like a phone. Miss one and they can leave a voicemail after your greeting: record your own, or have your words read by one of your server's voices.

Find your people

Servers can be open, take requests, or stay invite-only. Open ones are listed so anyone on your Llamble can look around and join.

1 · The app

Get Llamble for Windows and Android

The desktop app is the full experience: push-to-talk hotkeys, game-friendly screen sharing with sound, and notifications. The Android app has calls with the screen off and notifications while it's closed.

  1. Download and run Llamble-Setup.exe. On Android, download Llamble.apk and open it (allow installing from your browser when asked).
  2. Open the invite link you were sent, or type your server's address.
  3. That's it. The app keeps itself up to date.

2 · No install

Use it in your browser

On a phone, Chromebook or any computer, Llamble runs right in the browser: chat, voice and video included. On a phone, choose Add to Home Screen and it opens like an app.

Every Llamble server has its own web address. If someone invited you, just open their invite link; it takes you to the right place.

Go to your server

Type the address your server's owner gave you.

3 · Host it yourself

Run your own Llamble server

One container with everything inside: the server, the web app, voice and video (and the optional AI). It sets up its own storage. Create the admin account the first time you open it.

No NAS? Run it on a Windows PC. Llamble Server for Windows is the same server in a small tray app: install it, and it opens the setup page in your browser. Everyone else joins at the address it shows, while that PC is on.

Download Llamble Server for Windows

On a NAS or any Linux machine with Docker, paste one of these:

# TrueNAS: Apps → Discover Apps → ⋮ → Install via YAML → paste this services: llamble: image: ghcr.io/eeplanet/llamble:latest pull_policy: always restart: unless-stopped ports: - "8090:8080" # app + web client at home - "8443:8443" # HTTPS for remote access (if you turn it on) - "7881:7881" # voice/video (TCP) - "7882:7882/udp" # voice/video volumes: - llamble-data:/data volumes: llamble-data:
# Any Linux machine with Docker: save as docker-compose.yml, then `docker compose up -d` services: llamble: image: ghcr.io/eeplanet/llamble:latest pull_policy: always restart: unless-stopped ports: - "8090:8080" - "8443:8443" - "7881:7881" - "7882:7882/udp" volumes: - llamble-data:/data volumes: llamble-data:

Then open http://YOUR-SERVER-IP:8090 to finish setup. Remote access, accounts, the optional AI and everything else are set up from inside the app by the admin. The full guide is in the README.
